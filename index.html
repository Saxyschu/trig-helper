Find the other five.

You get one trig value and one clue about where θ is. Your job is the other five values. It's the same four moves every single time, and once you see them, this whole sheet is maybe 15 minutes.

The four moves

  1. Find the quadrant.The sign of the value you're given narrows it to two quadrants. The clue after the comma picks one.
  2. Draw the triangle.Put a right triangle in that quadrant. Read two sides straight off the fraction you were given, and give them the right signs for that quadrant.
  3. Find the missing side.Pythagoras: x² + y² = r². Then pick + or − for it based on the quadrant.
  4. Read off all six.Each trig function is just one side over another. Rationalize if there's a √ on the bottom.

Every side has a name

x is how far left or right (can be negative). y is how far up or down (can be negative). r is the slanted side, the distance from the center, so r is never negative.

The six ratios

sin θyrcsc θry
cos θxrsec θrx
tan θyxcot θxy

The right column is the left column flipped. Learn three, get six. (SOH CAH TOA is the same thing: opposite is y, adjacent is x, hypotenuse is r.)

Which ones are positive where? Tap a quadrant.

Memory trick, going counterclockwise from top right: All Students Take Calculus. All positive, then Sin, then Tan, then Cos (and their flips).

Two small things teachers check

Exact answers. Fractions and √, never decimals. −35, not −0.6.

No √ on the bottom. If you get 1√15, multiply top and bottom by √15: 1·√15√15·√15 = √1515. Same number, cleaner form.

Mistakes that cost points

  • Making r negative. The minus sign always goes on x or y.
  • Forgetting the sign on the side you found with Pythagoras. √ gives you the size, the quadrant gives you the sign.
  • Flipping a ratio (writing r/x when it's cos).

How to use this

Pick a problem up top. Each one walks you through the four moves, checks your answers as you go, and tells you why when something's off. Stuck? Hit Hint first, Show me second. Then do Practice until you can do one without hints, because the quiz won't have this page.

On an iPad: every problem has scratch paper at the bottom for the Apple Pencil, and the pink ✎ Scratch paper button opens it full screen. Your drawings stay put when you switch problems.
